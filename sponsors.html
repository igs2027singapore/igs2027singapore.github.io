Sponsors

IGS 2027 welcomes sponsorship from industry, government agencies, foundations, academic institutions, and other partners who wish to support the international applied geometry community.

Sponsorship Opportunities

Sponsorship contributions may support invited speakers, student travel awards, best-paper and poster awards, coffee breaks, social events, and other summit activities. All sponsors will be acknowledged on the IGS 2027 website, in selected summit announcements, during the summit, and through appropriate on-site or digital materials.

Sponsorship Levels

Platinum

US$15,000+

  • Recognition as a top-level sponsor of IGS 2027.
  • Opportunity for a sponsor presentation.
  • Opportunity to be named as the exclusive sponsor of one major summit activity or sponsorship opportunity.
  • Logo placement in the top sponsor tier on the summit website, programme materials, and appropriate on-site signage.
  • Opportunity to provide a limited number of approved materials or small items for distribution to attendees.
  • Four complimentary full summit registrations.
  • Opportunity to reserve a poster panel or display table during selected summit activities, subject to venue constraints.

Gold

US$5,000–US$14,999

  • Recognition as a major sponsor of IGS 2027.
  • Opportunity to be named as the sponsor of a coffee break, poster session, student-support activity, or other approved summit event.
  • Logo placement in the gold sponsor tier on the summit website, programme materials, and appropriate on-site signage.
  • Opportunity to provide a limited number of approved materials or small items for distribution to attendees.
  • Two complimentary summit registrations.
  • Opportunity to reserve a poster panel during selected summit activities, subject to venue constraints.

Silver

US$3,000–US$4,999

  • Recognition as a sponsor of IGS 2027.
  • Opportunity to be associated with a coffee break, workshop activity, student-support activity, or poster-session activity, subject to availability.
  • Logo placement in the silver sponsor tier on the summit website, programme materials, and appropriate on-site signage.
  • One complimentary summit registration.
  • Opportunity to reserve a poster panel during selected summit activities, subject to venue constraints.

Bronze

US$2,000–US$2,999

  • Recognition as a sponsor of IGS 2027.
  • Logo placement in the bronze sponsor tier on the summit website.
  • Acknowledgement in appropriate summit materials and on-site sponsor displays, where applicable.

Sponsorship benefits are subject to the final summit programme, registration policy, venue regulations, and approval by the organizers. The organizers may adjust recognition details where necessary to ensure consistency across the co-located partner conferences.

Sponsorship Contact

For sponsorship inquiries, please contact the Summit Co-Chair, Ying He, at yhe@ntu.edu.sg.
