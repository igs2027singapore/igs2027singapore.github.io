4th International Geometry Summit

June 21–25, 2027 Singapore

Marina Bay at dusk

The 4th International Geometry Summit (IGS 2027) will take place on June 21–25, 2027, at Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre. The summit will bring together the international applied geometry community for a week of co-located partner conferences, research exchange, and community meetings in Singapore.

Partner Conferences

IGS 2027 serves as a gateway to the participating conference communities. Each partner conference will maintain its own website for calls for papers, submission instructions, deadlines, and programme updates.

SGP

Eurographics Symposium on Geometry Processing

SGP, organized under the Eurographics Association, focuses on geometry-processing methods for digital 3D geometry, including algorithms, representations, analysis, manipulation, simulation, and applications.

SMI

Shape Modeling International

SMI provides an international forum for mathematical theories and computational techniques for modeling, simulating, processing, and analyzing digital shapes.

SPM

Symposium on Solid and Physical Modeling

SPM, organized under the Solid Modeling Association (SMA), covers geometric and physical modeling for design, analysis, simulation, manufacturing, biomedical applications, geophysics, and digital media.

GMP

International Conference on Geometric Modeling and Processing

GMP, organized under ASIAGRAPHICS, addresses mathematical and computational aspects of geometric modeling, processing, simulation, and computing, with applications in graphics, CAD/CAM, robotics, additive manufacturing, and scientific computing.

SIAM GD

SIAM Conference on Computational Geometric Design

SIAM GD, organized by the activity group of Geometric Design under SIAM, brings together mathematical and computational perspectives on generating, representing, and processing geometric information for engineering, design, manufacturing, and related applications.
